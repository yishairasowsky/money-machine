Why this exists

Consistency beats quality alone

Most content efforts die from missed weeks, not bad writing. A pre-built calendar removes the "what do I post today" decision.

Real drafts, not just topic lists

Not another "50 blog post ideas" list — each slot comes with an actual draft in a consistent, hook → points → action shape.

You stay in control

Nothing publishes itself. You review, fact-check, add your voice, and hit publish on your own accounts.

How it works

  1. Tell it your niche and posting cadence.
  2. Get a calendar of dated slots, each with a specific, searched-for topic.
  3. Get a draft per slot — hook, concrete points, one clear next action, ready for your edit pass.
  4. Publish on your schedule, then feed back what performed so the next batch gets sharper.

Who it's for

Solo creators, freelancers, and small businesses who know content marketing works but don't have hours a week to spend staring at a blank page deciding what to write about next.
