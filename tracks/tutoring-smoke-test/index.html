Why "before the test, not after"

Most tutoring starts too late

Families usually call after a bad grade. By then the class has moved on to three new topics built on the one that was shaky. Catching the gap in week 2, not week 8, is cheaper and less stressful for everyone.

Diagnose the mistake, not just the topic

"Bad at algebra" is rarely the real problem. It's usually one specific habit — a sign error, skipping a step, misreading the question — repeated across many problems. Fix the habit, not just today's homework.

What a session looks like

  1. Free 15-minute fit call — what class, what's shaky, what's coming up (test, unit, standardized test prep).
  2. Quick diagnostic — a handful of problems that reveal the actual mistake pattern, not just "review the chapter."
  3. Targeted 45–60 minute sessions, in person locally or online, focused on the 2–3 things actually causing lost points.
  4. A short written recap after each session — what we covered, what to practice, and a flag if something needs the classroom teacher's attention too.
