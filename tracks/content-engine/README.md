# Track B — Content engine

A repeatable process for turning one niche into a steady stream of publishable drafts, plus five real, ready-to-edit drafts to prove the process works. Nothing here posts anywhere automatically — you review and publish each piece yourself, on accounts this repo doesn't have access to.

**Niche chosen for this prototype:** personal finance for beginners (evergreen search demand, realistic affiliate/ad monetization paths, low topical risk). Swap the niche in `PROCESS.md` and re-run the process for anything else.

## What's here

- `PROCESS.md` — the repeatable method: how a topic becomes a calendar slot becomes a draft.
- `content_calendar.csv` — a 2-week posting schedule for the 5 drafts below plus 5 more open slots.
- `drafts/` — 5 real drafts, ready for you to fact-check, add your own voice/examples, and publish.

## Honest economics

Content income is not passive on day one — it's deferred effort: you front-load writing/editing now, and (if the audience shows up) it pays off with less marginal work later via affiliate links, ads, or your own product. Realistic timelines to meaningful income are usually months, not days, and most attempts don't reach meaningful traffic at all. Treat this as a real but slow-and-uncertain track, not the fast one.

## Next step to actually run this

1. Read and fact-check each draft — I generated them from general knowledge, not live sourcing, so verify any specific numbers/claims before publishing.
2. Pick where to publish (a blog, Medium, LinkedIn, etc.) — that's an account decision only you can make.
3. Publish on the calendar's cadence and see what gets traction before writing more.
