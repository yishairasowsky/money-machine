# The repeatable process

1. **Pick a niche** you can credibly write in and that has a plausible monetization path (affiliate products, ads, or your own future product). Write it down — it disciplines every topic choice after.
2. **Brainstorm topics** as *questions a beginner in this niche actually searches* ("how much emergency fund do I need"), not generic titles. Aim for specific, answerable questions over broad themes.
3. **Slot topics into a calendar** (`content_calendar.csv`) at a cadence you can sustain — twice a week beats daily-for-a-week-then-nothing.
4. **Draft each piece** to a fixed shape so quality doesn't wobble:
   - Hook: the specific problem/question, in one line.
   - 3-5 concrete, actionable points (numbers/examples, not platitudes).
   - One clear next action for the reader.
   - Honest about tradeoffs/risk — this is what separates useful content from noise.
5. **Human pass before publishing**: fact-check any specific numbers, add a real example or your own experience, adjust voice.
6. **Publish, then look at what performed** before writing the next batch — let real signal, not guesses, pick the next topics.

Re-run steps 2-4 for a new batch once the current calendar is close to empty.
